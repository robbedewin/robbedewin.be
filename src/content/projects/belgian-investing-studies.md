These grew out of the [financial planning engine](/projects/financial-planning-engine):
questions I kept needing a defensible answer to, where the international
literature exists but the Belgian version — with local costs, local taxes, and a
euro-denominated investor — mostly does not.

Each study is a standalone Quarto project with its own data pipeline, source
package, figures and tests, built on public datasets: Jordà–Schularick–Taylor
macrohistory, Shiller, Damodaran, Ken French, Goyal–Welch. They are written in
Dutch, because they were written for a Dutch-speaking reader.
